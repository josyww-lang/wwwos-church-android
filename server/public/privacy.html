Privacy policy

Oromo Stavanger Evangelical Church app · Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: the app is for the members and friends of our church. We use your details only to run the app: to sign you in, to let you chat and pray with each other, and to tell you about church life. We do not sell your information and the app has no advertising. You choose who can see your phone number and whether others see that you have read their messages. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run your account and the app (sign-in, chat, events, prayer, notifications). Basis: you agree when you create an account, and it is needed to give you the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and (b)).
  • To keep the community safe (approving members, reports, blocking, removing harmful messages). Basis: our legitimate interest in a safe church community (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Prayer requests and church membership can show religious belief, which is a special category of information. We use it only because you choose to share it in the app, and because the church is a non-profit religious body processing information about its own members and people who are in regular contact with it (Article 9(2)(a) and 9(2)(d)).

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not make automated decisions about you, and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see what

  • Chat: messages and photos in a group can be seen by the people in that group. Private messages can be seen by the two people in them. The church’s administrators can open conversations when they need to deal with a report or a safety concern.
  • Your name and profile photo are visible to other approved members.
  • Your phone number is visible only to the people you choose in each group (nobody, the group leader, or everyone in that group) and to the church’s administrators.
  • Read receipts: in private messages and groups of up to 50 people, the others can see that you have opened the conversation. Switch this off in the app under More (“Read receipts”); then you also stop seeing theirs.
  • Prayer requests: a request sent to “everyone” can be seen by approved members; a request sent to “leaders only” can be seen only by you and the administrators. If you post without your name, other members do not see it, but the church’s administrators may still be able to find out who sent it.
  • Event answers are used to show how many are coming. Feedback goes to the leaders.

5. Photos and sound files

A photo you send in chat is stored privately; only the people in that conversation and the church’s administrators can open it. A small preview of the photo is saved with the message. Deleting a message deletes its photo. Sermon recordings are added by the church’s leaders and are available to signed-in users.

6. Who we share information with

We do not share your information with anyone for their own purposes. To run the app we use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) as our service provider: sign-in, the database, file storage, notifications, and the small server that sends notifications. Google handles the information only for us and under its data-protection terms. We also use Firebase Crashlytics, so we can find and fix problems, and, once the app is on Google Play, Firebase App Check, which uses Google Play to check that the app is genuine and not a copy. The database and notification server are in Europe. Photos and sermon audio are kept in Google Cloud Storage in the United States. Transfers outside the EU/EEA rely on the safeguards Google provides (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

The Bible text. The Bible text in the reader and the verse of the day comes from online Bible services (API.Bible, and the YouVersion Platform for the Norwegian Bible). The app asks our server for a chapter, and the server asks the Bible service; the Bible service does not get your name, e-mail or any other detail about you. When you search for words in the Bible, the words you type are sent through our server to the Bible service to find the verses; we do not save them or connect them to you. Your coloured verses and your notes are private to you (see section 4).

Links in the app can open other services, such as YouTube (to watch a service live), Vipps, or a bank app. These are separate services with their own privacy policies. The app only shows our giving details; it does not handle payments.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we save your phone’s notification address so that new messages, announcements, events and live streams can reach you. You can switch notifications off in the app under More → Notifications, mute a single conversation, or turn them off in your phone settings. Your notification address is removed when you sign out or delete your account. A notification may show the start of a message on your lock screen.

The verse of the day at 9:00 (Norwegian time) is sent every morning unless you switch it off in the notification settings. For it, the app tells the notification server which language you use, together with your other notification choices. The verse can then be read on the lock screen.

8. How long we keep information

  • Your account details and profile photo: until you delete your account.
  • Your sermon notes, personal notes, Bible highlights, reading-plan progress, activity status, blocked list and scheduled messages: until you delete them or your account (they are removed when you delete your account).
  • Messages, photos, prayer requests and other things you posted: until you or an administrator delete them, or the group is deleted.
  • Reports about messages: until the leaders have dealt with them, and then deleted.

9. Your rights and choices

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to limit or object to how we use it, to receive a copy, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Contact us at waldaawwwos7@gmail.com.

  • Delete your account in the app: More → Delete account (at the very bottom). See also how account deletion works.
  • Edit your name, phone number and photo: tap your profile picture at the top right.
  • Control what others see: phone-number sharing per group, read receipts, notifications and muting are all in the app.

If you think we handle your information wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet): www.datatilsynet.no.

10. Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use chat with the knowledge of your parents or guardians. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. Leaders can remove messages and members, and every member can report a message or block a person.

11. Security

Information travels between the app and our services in encrypted form. Access to information is limited by rules in the database: for example, only the people in a conversation can read it, and only administrators can post announcements. Administrators are chosen by the church leaders. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and do not share it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
