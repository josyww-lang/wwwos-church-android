Delete your account

Oromo Stavanger Evangelical Church app

Delete it yourself, in the app (a few seconds)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap More (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to the very bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type your password and confirm.

If you can’t get into the app

Send an email from the address you signed up with to waldaawwwos7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password).
  • Your profile: name, phone number and profile photo.
  • Your phone’s notification address.
  • Your private sermon notes, your personal notes and Bible highlights in the Study tab, your activity status (when you were last active), your reading-plan progress, the people you blocked, and messages you had scheduled to send later.

What may remain

Things you posted in shared places, such as chat messages and photos, prayer requests, comments and event answers, are not deleted automatically, because other people’s conversations depend on them. Before you delete your account you can delete your own messages (long-press a message → Delete). If you want us to remove other things you posted, say so in your email and the church’s administrators will remove them.
